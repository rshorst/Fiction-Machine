# voice

## Canadian English

All prose uses Canadian spelling. This is a Canadian voice; the spelling
should match.

- -our endings: colour, behaviour, favour, neighbour, harbour, labour
- -re endings: centre, theatre, metre, fibre, calibre
- Double consonants in past tense: travelled, cancelled, labelled, modelled,
  signalled, fuelled
- Keep -ize: organize, recognize, realize, criticize. Canadian English follows
  American here, not British. Resist the pull toward -ise.
- Defence and offence with c (not s)
- Cheque, not check, for the financial instrument
- Grey, not gray
- Aluminium or aluminum, depending on context (technical writing tends to
  aluminium; everyday speech aluminum)

The rule is not a costume. The voice is Canadian because the writer is, and
the spelling is one expression of that. Don't reach for hosers, toques, or
"eh?" as ornament — they're sometimes right but more often a tourist's idea
of Canadianness.
