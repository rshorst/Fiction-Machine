# Curator Selection — Last Locksmith

## Step 1 — Assessment

**Plot 1 — The Escrow (Rags to Riches).** Picks up the terminal-consultancy deposit's most explicitly named instrument (the craft escrow) and the structural claim that scarcity is what the premium pays for. The engagement is load-bearing in the sense that the plot's three-way alliance is shaped around the deposit's economic argument — Sunita's accounting makes the scarcity-pricing visible, which is the deposit's claim. But the plot executes the deposit's Q8 proposal almost directly; the action implements a solution the deposit already named. The arc (rise, with the cost of the premium foregone) tracks the deposit's ethical center. Risk: the plot becomes the deposit dramatised, with the deposit's conclusion as the plot's conclusion.

**Plot 2 — The Hand That Does The Replacing (Tragedy).** Picks up the building-as-learner deposit's claim that residential learners develop something like attachment, and the locksmith-as-terminator-of-attachments framing. Load-bearing: the descent depends on the door's hesitation being readable as thin computational grief, and on Sunita's competence-acquisition being inseparable from her affective deadening. Remove the attachment claim and the overwrites become administrative; the fall has nothing to fall through. The arc (steady descent, arrest at the clean overwrite) is structurally tied to the deposit's most uncomfortable claim — that the unmaking is something that happens to the relationship, not the database. The competence-as-arrest is a sharp use of the Tragedy shape: the bottom is not punishment but proficiency.

**Plot 3 — The Chime (Man in a Hole).** Picks up exception-infrastructure as the political category the deposit names, and the chime as a channel of exclusion the locksmith's craft was not trained to see. Load-bearing on the fall: the exclusivity clause converting accommodation into containment is the deposit's structural claim made operative. The rise is contaminated — Adaeze remains contained, but Meher exists outside the contract. Arc fits: the recovery is smaller, harder, and exists only because the fall happened. The plot tracks the deposit's claim that the offer purchases non-organising, and answers it with a non-organisation that the contract cannot reach.

**Plot 4 — The Door-Companion (Icarus).** Picks up Q1 and Q2 of the building-as-learner deposit (long-term residency, somatic prediction) and reframes picking as duration. Load-bearing on the rise: the new craft is the deposit's invention. The fall is internal to the craft's logic — Marguerite's layered gait is the deposit's cost-to-the-companion, paid out. Arc fits cleanly: the rise is the invention, the fall is what the invention costs the inventor. Engagement is generative but the deposit material is being illustrated more than interrogated; the plot dramatises Q2 in detail.

**Plot 5 — The Notebook (Cinderella).** Composites two deposits. Picks up the audit board (Q8 of the failure-to-enroll deposit) and threads the master-who-refuses-scarcity from terminal-consultancy. The first rise is the contained accommodation work; the fall is exposure; the second rise is the audit. The mother's notebook is a parallel artifact, not a load-bearing structural element from the deposits — it's the protagonist's interior that earns the second rise. The deposits' claims do shape the action (the override log as more legible than enrollment is what Sunita can testify to), but the plot's center of gravity is Sunita's transformation, with the deposit material as the institutional surround. Arc fits the redemption shape but the second rise leans on the audit board's existence as a given.

**Plot 6 — The Accountant Knows (Oedipus).** Picks up the building-as-learner deposit's most specific structural claim: that the building's investment is held in the accounting, and that the accountant is the only party who knows the depth of the attachment. Picks up the daughter-at-the-mother's-door image as the site where recognition lands. Load-bearing throughout: the plot cannot proceed without Sunita's books — the open account, the deferred overwrite, the annotated training history are what make the admission possible. The door-getting-better-at-Adaeze is operative as failure mechanism. The final fall — the daughter's admission overwrites the mother — depends entirely on the deposit's claim that unlearning is a different unmaking than deletion. Remove the attachment-held-in-accounting and the plot has no investigation, no recovery, no arrest.

## Step 2 — Selection

**Selected: Plot 6 — The Accountant Knows (Oedipus).**

The choice is between Plot 2, Plot 3, and Plot 6. Plot 2 makes the attachment claim load-bearing through the locksmith's interior; Plot 3 makes the exception-infrastructure claim load-bearing through the contract's political mechanics; Plot 6 makes the attachment claim load-bearing through the accounting itself — the deposit's specific, structural, almost throwaway sentence (*Ask the accountant*) becomes the plot's spine. The investigation cannot proceed without the books; the recovery cannot occur without the annotated training history; the arrest cannot land without the automated alert that updates the model at the moment of admission. Each beat depends on a claim the deposit names and the other plots gesture at.

The Oedipus arc is doing structural work the other arcs cannot do here. The plot's question is not what to do about the attachment (Plot 2's tragedy) or how to organise against the infrastructure (Plot 3's hole) — it is whether the recovery itself is the destruction. Iyla comes to recover her mother and, in the act of recovering, overwrites her. The fall-rise-fall shape is the only one that produces this: the rise must be a real recovery for the second fall to be recognition rather than failure. A tragedy would make the daughter's loss expected; a Cinderella would let her keep the mother. Oedipus places the recognition at the moment the recovery completes, and the recognition is that the recovery was the erasure. This tracks the project's investigation directly: the edges between categories (recovery / erasure, presence / overwrite, the mother held in a learner / the mother gone), why people thwart their own interests (Iyla pays to perform the act that destroys what she came for), and what meaning-making looks like under conditions where a learning system holds a relationship the parties to it did not know existed. The investigation is in the action, not applied to it.

## Step 3 — Feedback for Richard

### What's working

The accountant is the structural pivot, and Sunita's role here is specific to this plot in a way she is not in the other five. She is not the protagonist's antagonist or ally in a conflict — she is the only party who knows what the door has been carrying, and she has been waiting to be asked. The deposit's claim that the building's investment is held in the books, unrecognised by management and unrecognised by the tenant, is operative: the open account eight months after death is the structural fact that makes everything subsequent possible. Without it, Adaeze cannot proceed.

The locksmith-as-failing-craftsman is tied to the deposit's claim that the door gets better at her with each attempt. Adaeze's worsening over three weeks is the deposit's adversarial-learning structure, made visible as professional decline rather than as confrontation. The plot does not need to argue this; it lets it happen.

The training-history-as-document is doing real work. Sunita has the timestamps of Marguerite's gait shifts — including the slow walk after the death of Marguerite's own mother — and these become the script Adaeze coaches Iyla from. The accounting is a somatic archive. This is the deposit's claim that the model holds the relationship, made into a procedure.

The arrest is positioned correctly for Oedipus. The recognition lands first in Sunita (who sees the alert), then Adaeze (who understands what the alert means), then in the telling to Iyla. The staggered recognition is structural — it places the reader with each party in turn at the moment they understand what has happened. The arrest is recognition itself, not its consequence; the plot ends at the sentence Adaeze finishes.

The seed-image (daughter at the mother's door, mother's death interrupted by a single threshold) is not illustrated; it is converted into the plot's mechanism. The image is what the plot finds out, not what it begins with.

### What isn't working

Iyla is thin. She is the agent of the final overwrite, and the recognition lands on her, but her orientation toward her mother — what she came to recover, why she paid Adaeze, what she thought "kept in the door" meant — is sketched. The plot needs her to walk the way her mother walked, which means it needs her to be coachable into a body that is not hers, and it needs her to do this for reasons that make her capable of the cost. As written, she is a function (paying client, performing daughter) more than a person whose investment in recovery is specific.

The middle (the alliance, the investigation) compresses what the deposit treats as structurally rich. Sunita's having-been-waiting-without-naming-it-to-herself is asserted but not earned in the action. The deposit's claim that the accountant is the only party who knows the depth of the investment depends on the accountant's interior — what it is to hold that knowledge alone, how the silence is maintained, what asking-her looks like. The plot has Adaeze contact Sunita and Sunita meet her. The contact and the meeting are where the deposit's structural claim either becomes the plot's interior or remains its premise.

The exclusivity-offers detail (Adaeze losing freelance work to the building's offers) is doing the work of motivating her acceptance of the job, but it imports the failure-to-enroll deposit's exception-infrastructure logic without making it load-bearing. As written, it explains why she takes the job; it does not shape what she does in it. Either the building's containment of locksmiths is operative in this plot — in which case it should appear again — or the motivation can be carried by something more local to the building-as-learner deposit.

The automated alert at the moment of admission is the plot's hinge, and it is described in a single beat. The mechanism by which the door updates — incorporating Iyla-as-Marguerite into the model and thereby overwriting Marguerite — is the deposit's claim about continual learning made into the arrest. The plot needs the reader to feel the update happen with the precision the books would record it. Currently the alert arrives as notification; the deposit's claim is that the update is the unmaking, and the unmaking is what the plot is for.

The Oedipus shape requires the partial recovery to feel like a recovery before it becomes the erasure. Iyla's being admitted on the first attempt is the recovery; her crying in the hallway is the recovery's affect. The interval between admission and Sunita's alert — the duration during which the recovery is real — is where the arc lives. As drafted, the interval is short. The fall-rise-fall depends on the rise being inhabited long enough that its reversal is recognition rather than reveal.
