**LONG-DISTANCE DEPENDENCIES**

The arrest at ¶124 (Sunita and Adaeze carrying the gait) requires that the reader has been taught, before the door scene, that bodies absorb what they watch. The draft has not taught this. Adaeze says it (¶53) as instruction to Iyla, but she says it about Iyla and her mother — a relationship the reader accepts as given. The draft never shows Adaeze or Sunita absorbing anything before the moment they have absorbed it. The setup needs to be quieter and earlier: Adaeze, watching Iyla walk in the corridor, shifting weight in the same rhythm without noticing. One sentence at ¶64. Then ¶124 is paid for.

The single-entry overwrite (Miranda's flag) is the load-bearing mechanism the draft has not justified. The exposition at ¶78 makes the unjustified rate visible. Two options: justify it (high learning rate to track gait-drift in aging tenants — Sunita can say this in two sentences at ¶37), or move to slow overwrite (Iyla would have to walk through repeatedly, knowing each walk takes more). The slow version is the harder, truer story. The single-entry version is the one currently on the page. Decide.

The mechanism inconsistency at ¶111 (the door closes on a timer like a powered door, then is described throughout as a latching door with a strike) is a digitality channel failure that costs the draft credibility on the technical register the rest of it has earned. Pick the mechanism and rewrite the closing of the door to match.

## ARC LEVEL

**Arc:** Icarus, with the rise compressed into the climb to the threshold and the fall arriving in the same gesture as the rise — the admission *is* the overwriting. The draft is not resisting the shape; it is collapsing the two halves onto a single moment, which is the structural move that earns it.

Arrest requires that ¶124 stand alone, that the explanation in ¶125 be cut, and that the closer not reach past it. The draft is moving toward arrest and then walking past it for two more paragraphs.
