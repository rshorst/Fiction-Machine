# Curator Selection — creative_debt

## Step 1 — Assessment of each plot

**Plot 1 — The Counterfactual Studio (Rags to Riches).** Picks up the misgeneralization Q3 (counterfactual studio, the proxies) and the ecology paper's residency mechanic. The pickup is load-bearing on the rise: the work is good *because* the process is producing the Megan-she-no-longer-is, and the rent climbs *because* her residents have become load-bearing to others. Removing either deposit changes what happens. The arrest depends on both: she cannot stop because the ecology has woven her in, and she cannot stop because the work people praise is the proxy. Project ethos: the question of why people thwart their interests is structurally present — the trap is dressed as triumph, the rise is the mechanism of the misgeneralization. Rise as arc shape does real work: the upward trajectory is what makes the late recognition unavailable to her in time.

**Plot 2 — The Cheap Tier (Tragedy).** Picks up the provenance-graded compute speculation (Q8) and the recursion mechanic. Engagement is load-bearing: the entire fall is structured by the cheap-tier mechanism, and the arrest specifically requires the meter-measures-the-wrong-thing claim. The Tragedy arc lines up with the irreversibility the paper insists on. Project ethos: tracks the substrate question (what is the work drawing from) and the visibility question (the maker cannot see the contamination) cleanly. The risk is that the speculative tier-conspiracy carries a lot of weight; if the reader doesn't accept the infrastructure premise, the fall reads as poor luck rather than structural inevitability.

**Plot 3 — The Drift Detector (Man in a Hole).** Picks up Q8 of the misgeneralization deposit (drift detector as product). The detector is the rise mechanism, so it is load-bearing in one sense — but the detector is also a near-literal staging of the deposit. The plot's central insight (the practice was proxy-managing the relationship) does not require the deposit's specific mechanism; it could be told about a man with a journal habit. The deposit is illustrated rather than worked. Arc commitment (Man in a Hole) does its job — the rise is contaminated by what the recovery cost — but the contamination is interpersonal rather than structural to the theory.

**Plot 4 — Resident (Icarus).** Picks up the ecology paper's Q1/Q2 (world-share, residency, becoming-substrate) and its closing claim about indispensability. Load-bearing throughout: the rise is ecological, the fall is the rent mechanism, the arrest is the substrate-not-customer line worked into action. Removing the deposit dissolves the plot. Icarus as arc is structurally apt — the rise contains the fall because the same mechanism produces both. Project ethos: tracks meaning-as-ecology and the edges-between-categories question (maker/substrate, customer/material) directly. Misgeneralization is named as a tonal note but does no work.

**Plot 5 — Two Ascents (Cinderella).** Layers collapse and misgeneralization deliberately. Both deposits are load-bearing in the fall — the rejection comment names both, and the plot insists they cannot be told apart. The second rise picks up Q5 of the misgeneralization deposit (the structural account of self-thwarting) and turns the proxy into archive-against-which-to-write. Cinderella as arc commitment is doing real work: the second rise is structurally different from the first, not a restoration. Project ethos: the project's question about why people thwart their interests is held in the action of the fall, and the meaning-making question is held in the second rise's mechanism (writing against the proxy). The "two pressures collide" move is the plot's strongest theoretical commitment — it refuses to let either deposit do the work alone.

**Plot 6 — The Footnote No One Cites (Oedipus).** Picks up Q4 of the misgeneralization deposit at maximum recursion. Load-bearing: every turn is the deposit's mechanism applied at a deeper level. The arc (fall-rise-fall) is the recursion made structural. Project ethos: tracks the question of self-knowledge under conditions where the apparatus of self-knowledge is itself drifting — directly. The risk is closure: the recursion has no exit by design, and the plot's commitment to that closure means the action reduces to one move performed three times. The deposit is fully worked but at the cost of structural variety.

## Step 2 — Selection

**Selected: Plot 5 — Two Ascents (Cinderella).**

Plot 5 is the only plot that puts both load-bearing deposits into structural collision and makes the collision the plot's engine. The fall is not collapse-or-misgeneralization; it is the maker's inability to distinguish them in real time, and her attempt to pay her way out makes both worse simultaneously. The rejection comment — "this reads like a younger writer flattening into average" — is one sentence that holds both deposits at once: *younger writer* is the misgeneralization (the proxy), *flattening into average* is the collapse (the thinning toward the mean). Removing either deposit changes the plot. Removing both ends it.

The Cinderella arc is the right structural commitment because the project's investigation — where meaning emerges, why people thwart their interests, how technology might help us know ourselves — requires a shape in which the second rise is *not* a restoration. Rise-fall-rise lets the plot enact the misgeneralization deposit's quietest claim: that recognising drift can become a creative resource if the maker is honest about what the proxy was. Megan does not recover the project; she writes against its outputs, using them as antagonist. This is the project's investigation made structural: meaning emerges not by retrieval but by writing in correspondence with what the apparatus produced instead of what she meant. The arrest — the paragraph about her mother that the Megan-who-began would not recognize — is the project's question about the edges between categories worked into a single sentence: she has become someone the proxy could not have produced, and the proof is that the proxy is now legible to her as something separate from herself. Plot 4 tracks the ecology question very directly, and Plot 6 works the misgeneralization deposit at the deepest single-deposit level, but neither does what Plot 5 does, which is hold the deposits in tension and let the maker's contradiction (accommodating scorekeeper) do load-bearing work in both phases — weaponised against her in the fall, weaponised by her in the second rise.

## Step 3 — Feedback for Richard

### What's working

- The two-deposits-at-once move in the fall is the plot's structural core. The rejection sentence — *this reads like a younger writer flattening into average* — does real work: it is one observation that contains both deposits and refuses to let Megan separate them. The "she tries to pay her way out — premium tier, larger model" sequence is the meter-measures-the-wrong-thing claim worked into action; both deposits predict that paying more accelerates the failure, and the plot makes this happen on the page.

- Megan's contradiction (accommodating scorekeeper) carries different load in each phase. In the rise and fall, the scorekeeping is what makes her good at producing the proxy — she calculates her early aesthetic with precision she would not apply to her current taste. In the second rise, the same scorekeeping is what lets her use the proxy outputs as archive — she can score them coldly because she is no longer trying to be them. The contradiction is not decorative; it is the mechanism by which the same person can be the victim of the proxy and the maker who writes against it.

- Tobias's function across the arc is structurally distinct in each phase. In the rise he is the reader who confirms the proxy. In the fall he sends the kindest and least useful paragraph he has ever written — the warm wall failing exactly where warmth was the problem. In the second rise he disagrees, which is the first thing he has done that the proxy could not predict. The character's contradiction (generous opacity) is being used as a measuring instrument for Megan's drift.

- The second rise's mechanism — non-fiction with the collapsed-process outputs as appendix and antagonist — is the misgeneralization deposit's Q3 read against itself. The deposit speculates about commissioning misgeneralized versions of oneself as art; the plot has Megan discover she has accidentally produced one and writes against it. This is the project's investigation made structural: the apparatus did not give her meaning, but the failure of the apparatus is the material from which meaning becomes available.

- The Adrien turn — "she tells him no, on a small thing, and means it" — does not resolve the relationship and does not redeem him. It registers a small fact that the proxy would have foreclosed. This is the project's question about why people thwart their interests held in a single sentence: the accommodating Megan was the one the proxy could keep producing; the Megan who can say no is the one who has stepped outside the optimisation.

- The arrest — the paragraph about the mother, finished alone, not told — refuses the publication-as-resolution shape. The second rise lands in private knowledge of a structural change, not in external confirmation. This is the right scale for what the plot is claiming.

### What isn't working

- The collapse mechanics are asserted more than enacted. We are told the substrate is thinning, but the plot does not show the reader the texture of the thinning the way it shows the misgeneralization (through Tobias's "feels like the old work"). The fall depends on the reader accepting that both pressures are present; one of them is currently doing more work than the other on the page. The "checkbox she did not see" mechanism from Plot 2 is not available here — Plot 5 has chosen a different fall — so the collapse needs a different way of being legible to the reader inside Megan's experience.

- The rejection sentence is doing a lot of work as the moment that names both deposits. It is the right kind of move, but as currently staged it arrives from outside (a press's comment) and Megan's reading of it is summarised. The plot's central claim — that she cannot tell which problem is which — needs a scene in which the indistinguishability is something Megan does, not something she is told.

- The second rise's mechanism is named at the right level of abstraction (writing against the proxy, archive-as-antagonist) but the plot does not specify what the writing actually does with the outputs. The misgeneralization deposit's Q3 is a precise idea — the work the artist would have made on a road they did not take — and the second rise could be more committed to what it means to use those specific outputs as material. Right now "in conversation with the proxy" is a claim; the action of the conversation is thin.

- The ecology paper is mentioned as almost entirely set aside, which is a clean derivation choice, but the "small ecology" of readers around the chapbook in the rise is doing some of the ecology paper's work without admitting it. Either the rise is purely about her process and her readers (in which case the ecology paper is genuinely left aside) or the small ecology is structurally relevant (in which case the derivation should say so). The current state has the deposit working quietly in a place the derivation says it isn't.

- Adrien's role across the arc is less specified than Tobias's. He is "indifferent but supportive" in the rise, "never read the work" in the fall, and the partner who asks to be told no in the second rise. The third move is the strongest of the three; the first two are positions rather than functions. If the no-saying scene is going to land as the project's question about thwarting one's interests, the prior Adrien needs to have been doing something specific that the no is a departure from.

- The arc's middle — the bottom — is the shortest section in the derivation, and it is carrying the transition between the two rises. The bottom is where the collapse and the misgeneralization both have to be undeniable to Megan, and where the decision to use the outputs as archive has to become available rather than chosen. As currently sketched, the bottom is one paragraph of summary; the plot's whole structural claim depends on what happens in those pages.
