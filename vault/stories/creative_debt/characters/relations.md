# Relations

## Relational web

Megan and Elliot live in the same city and have probably been in the same room three or four times without registering each other. The wine bar on Dundas West where Elliot works is on a route Megan walks home from a friend's place in Little Portugal. She has eaten there once, with Adrien, in 2022. Elliot was on shift. He poured her wine. She tipped 22% and remembered nothing about him except that he had a hat on indoors and she had a small private opinion about that. He remembered her — not by name — as the woman with the boyfriend who looked at the menu like she was reading a memo. He noticed the way she was with the boyfriend. He thought: she doesn't actually like him. He was wrong, sort of. He was right about something.

Tobias and Megan met once, at a wedding in Kenora in 2022 — Megan's university roommate married Tobias's cousin. They sat at the same table for the dinner. They talked for forty minutes about Manitoba versus Ontario winters and about a book they had both read. Megan thought he was the kindest person she'd met all year and slightly suspect for it. Tobias thought she was sharper than the room and that she was watching him the way he watches other people, which unnerved him more than he showed. They added each other on Instagram. Neither has messaged. Megan looks at his stories. Tobias has noticed she looks at his stories.

Tobias and Elliot don't know each other. They have one connection neither would discover unprompted: Felix, Elliot's roommate, played in a band that opened for a Winnipeg act Tobias once drove three hours to see. They were in the same room for an hour in 2019. Nothing passed.

What sits underneath: all three were the easy child. All three are managing a parent who isn't being told the truth. All three would recognize the others' specific exhaustion within a minute of conversation, and none of them would name it. Megan would clock Elliot's hat and Tobias's smile as the same object in different materials. Neither man would clock hers as quickly. Desire, where it sits: Megan and Tobias, latent — the wedding-table forty minutes, the Instagram looking, the not-messaging. Nothing has happened. Something is held. Elliot would find Megan intimidating and would want her to find him interesting and would not get his way. Tobias would find Elliot tiring within ten minutes.

## Possible collisions

Megan wants to be loved by someone who could see her being a little cruel and not flinch. Tobias is the most flinch-prone of the three — kind, careful, raised to absorb sharpness as if it were aimed at God. He could not give her what she's looking for. He could, however, mistake her for the answer to his own unstated desire, which is to be wanted in a way that doesn't feel like being chosen for a role. Megan choosing him would feel exactly like being chosen for a role. The pressure is real and it would not survive contact.

Elliot wants to be looked at without setting the terms. Megan is one of the few people who would look at him without his terms working — she reads rooms faster than he performs them. He would feel exposed and call it chemistry. She would feel mildly entertained and call it nothing.

Tobias's contradiction — warm wall, generous opacity — is dangerous to anyone who needs him to be uncomplicated. Erin needs that. Megan would not. Megan's contradiction — accommodating scorekeeper — is dangerous to Adrien, whose only request is to be agreed with. Elliot's contradiction — engineering the invisibility he resents — is dangerous to himself, mostly. He is not yet equipped to be dangerous to anyone else, which is one of the things he would like to learn and is afraid to.

## What each is to each

**Megan, on Tobias:** A man being good at being good. I liked him. I don't trust the smile and I don't think he does either, which is the only reason I keep looking at his stories.
**Tobias, on Megan:** Someone who saw me at that wedding the way I see kids in my class. I don't think I want to be seen like that. I think I want to be seen exactly like that.

**Elliot, on Megan:** The kind of woman who would not be impressed by me, which is the kind of woman I keep saying I want and keep avoiding.
**Megan, on Elliot:** A boy in a hat. I've poured a thousand glasses of wine for boys in hats. I don't remember his face.

**Tobias, on Elliot:** [they have not met. If they did:] I'd be polite to him for ninety minutes and exhausted for the rest of the day.
**Elliot, on Tobias:** [they have not met. If they did:] Teacher. Nice guy. Probably from somewhere flat. I'd buy him a drink and not remember his name.
