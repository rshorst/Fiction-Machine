## ARC LEVEL

**Arc:** Icarus, refused into something else. The rise is the platform tier escalation and the Tobias correspondence; the fall is the editor's note and the *Training & Provenance* checkbox. The draft refuses Icarus by making the fall the condition for a different kind of seeing — not punishment, not lesson, but the machine functioning as diagnostic instrument for the human misgeneralizations already running in the room. What it proposes instead: the fall as the apparatus that makes legible what was already broken and unbilled.

**Position:** The fall has happened. The recognition has happened. The proofs are in the recycling. The door has clicked. The draft is now in the scene after arrest, asking Adrien to perform the asking — and getting it. This is the danger zone: the draft has already arrested at ¶31 (proofs in bin, *passed through the place where she could have gone back*) and is continuing past it.

**Arrest requires:** The thing that must become irreversible is the registering, with herself, that she is not going to be that writer — and that registering is complete at ¶31. Everything after ¶31 is either the cost of the arrest made visible (the meter still running, the unsent cancellation) or a second resolution being layered on. The Adrien scene is doing the latter. It wants to give the arrest a partner. The arrest does not need a partner. It needs the meter running in the unclosed tab and the door clicking and nothing further.

**Flag:** Cut the Adrien-asks-and-she-tells scene, or cut it down to the looking. The arrest is at ¶31. The *Ask me what it's about* sequence resolves the marriage-channel cleanly when the marriage-channel is one of Miranda's three loops and should remain in the same unresolved state as the Tobias channel and the platform channel (meter running, no cancellation). The draft has earned the right to leave Adrien in the kitchen not asking. Letting him ask, and her tell, and him receive — even imperfectly — gives the marriage a hopeful trajectory the other two loops are denied. Three loops, three time-constants, one mechanism: keep the symmetry. The arrest is that she has become someone she cannot report to anyone in the apartment. Do not then have her report it.
