ARC LEVEL

**Arc:** Resisting the inheritance-plot Rise (woman gets business, finds secret, accumulates knowledge). Proposing instead an Oedipus shape stalled at the front edge — fall into knowledge that the protagonist is choosing not to complete. The story is about a woman inheriting her father's method of not-knowing and reproducing it on the same materials.

**Position:** First descent. She has opened the folder, read the unsent letter, held the untranslatable word and chosen not to translate it, read the photograph caption she does know. Voss has been named on the phone. Nothing irreversible has happened — every act so far is a gesture she could still walk back: she could close the folder, fly to Cologne, hand the file to a lawyer.

**Arrest requires:** That her not-knowing become the same act as her father's not-knowing — that the choice not to look up the word, or the choice not to call Voss back, or the choice to put the folder back spine-down, become the moment she cannot un-choose. The draft is moving toward it. The Voss call is the wrong vehicle for it (external pressure forces the choice, which makes it plot, not character). The right vehicle is already in the draft: the word she did not look up. That refusal needs to harden into a structural fact about who she now is.

**Flag:** The Voss phone call is being set up to do the arrest's work and it shouldn't. Voss arriving turns the story from "the daughter inherits the architecture" into "the daughter is pursued by a stranger." The wandering cost: the draft keeps reaching for external escalation when the irreversible thing is interior and already in the room. The arrest is the untranslated word, not the lawyer.
