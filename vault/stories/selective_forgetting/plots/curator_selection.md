# Curator Selection

## Step 1 — Assessment of the six plots

**Plot 1 — The Shadow Index (Rags to Riches).** Picks up *withholding* and the propagation-to-successor passage from 2511.04666 and 2512.13706. The engagement is load-bearing in the propagation mechanic: Margit's arrest depends on the practice having become non-centralized, which is the deposit's claim about counter-archives that accumulate into tradition. The rise arc tracks the deposit's reframing — what looked like sabotage is revealed as construction. Risk: the propagation happens off-stage in Marisol's head, and the leap from audit-conversation to Marisol replicating the practice in a different country compresses the costliest part of the theory into a letter.

**Plot 2 — The Promise (Tragedy).** Picks up catastrophic forgetting / specialization-as-forgetting from 2512.13706. Load-bearing: the fall is structurally produced by the paper's claim — Margit is finetuned alongside the system, loses the general capacity, and her final signing is the consequence of that loss. The arc shape does real work because the deposit predicts irreversibility ("the trade-off is irreversible within standard training") and tragedy is the arc that respects irreversibility. The 1962 hearing / automated notification is a clean dramatization of "retrieval-fluency overwrites refusal-capacity." This is the plot where removing the theory removes the plot.

**Plot 3 — Not This Drawer (Man in a Hole).** Picks up curriculum-rewriting and *withholding*, but the rise after the fall is driven by the brother-disclosure, not by the theoretical material. The archive practice "continues, unmentioned, in the background." The deposits set up the situation but the structural turn is interpersonal honesty. Theory is load-bearing for the setup, decorative for the arrest.

**Plot 4 — Dialect (Icarus).** Picks up the science-fictional speculation from 2503.21676 — the AI learning the misfilings as ground truth, structured hallucinations as signature. Load-bearing: the rise *is* the system learning the dialect; the fall *is* re-ingestion flattening it. The arc shape matches the deposit's phase-transition framing — non-smooth jumps in acquisition and loss. The peak (Elin's recognition) and the fall (engineers identifying contamination) are produced by the theoretical claim that misfilings compose new neighborhoods the system reads. Removing the theory removes the plot.

**Plot 5 — The Letter Ruth Wrote Back (Cinderella).** Picks up the cost-of-competence and *withholding-as-relation*. Load-bearing in the fall: Marisol's collapse is structurally produced by the deposit's claim that the practice was safe for one specialized custodian and lethal transmitted out. But the second rise pivots on Margit calling her father — the theory does not produce the recovery, character does. Theory load-bearing for the fall, decorative for the rise.

**Plot 6 — The Hands That Knew (Oedipus).** Picks up *I do not know if she knows this* and the pre-articulate intuition. The recognition reframes the misfiling as self-protection rather than theoretical resistance. The arc lands on seeing, but what is seen is psychological rather than structural-theoretical. The deposits supply the situation; the recognition is about Marisol's avoidance pattern. Theory is the occasion, not the engine.

## Step 2 — Selection

**Selected: Plot 2 — The Promise (Tragedy / Riches to Rags).**

Plot 2 is the plot where the theoretical claim is the plot's engine. The deposit on catastrophic forgetting says specialization destroys general capacity and the trade-off is irreversible. Plot 2 takes that claim and makes it the mechanism of the fall: Margit's endorsement triggers ingestion; ingestion finetunes the system *and her*; her hands lose the knack; the 1962 hearing surfaces because the discretion that would have refused the surfacing has been trained out of both her and the system; the final signing is the recognition that her refusal would now be incoherent. Each step is the deposit's claim instantiated in action. Remove the theory and there is no fall — just an aging archivist regretting a contract.

The arc commitment is the right structural choice because the deposit's strongest claim is irreversibility. Rags-to-Riches (Plot 1) and Cinderella (Plot 5) require the practice to remain available; Man in a Hole (Plot 3) requires recovery; Icarus (Plot 4) and Oedipus (Plot 6) require a peak or a recognition that reorganizes the field. Tragedy is the only arc that lets the deposit's irreversibility do its full work — the fall is not punished, not redeemed, not reframed; it completes. Project ethos lands here because the investigation — why people thwart their own interests, where meaning emerges under conditions of AI synthesis, what is lost when retrieval becomes the dominant relation to the past — is structurally present in the action. Margit signs because she has been made into someone whose refusal would mean nothing. That is the project's central question dramatized as plot mechanics, not as subject matter. Plot 4 is the closest competitor and engages 2503.21676 with comparable load-bearing precision, but its arc resolves into an aestheticized loss; Plot 2's arc resolves into a person who has been changed by her own competence into someone she would not have endorsed.

## Step 3 — Feedback for Richard

### What's working

- The endorsement at the start is doing structural work. Margit's professional standing is what makes the contract possible; her standing is then what gets dismantled by the contract she enabled. The fall is self-implicating in a way the deposit's "specialization is forgetting" predicts but does not require — you have made her the cause of her own finetuning.
- The "she has stopped touching the paper" detail locates the cost-of-competence in the body. The deposit talks about general capability degrading; the plot finds the specific bodily site where that degradation registers. This is the deposit instantiated, not illustrated.
- The attempt to misfile late, after reading something she will not name, and finding her hands have lost the knack — this is the clearest dramatization of catastrophic forgetting in any of the six plots. The capacity is gone before she notices it is gone, which is what the paper actually claims.
- Elin's appearance is doing precise structural work. The 1962 hearing is not just a sad event; it is the moment the protocol the system was optimized to perform performs itself, and Margit sees that the discretion she thought was institutional was her. The automated notification routes the consequence outside the archive walls, which is the deposit's "no custodian's hands left to compensate" made operational.
- The signing at the end holds because it is not a failure of will. You have made her sign from a recognition that her refusal would be incoherent — she is no longer the person whose refusal would mean anything. This respects the deposit's irreversibility claim. The arc does not soften.
- The candle she does not light to pray, in a church she has not been to in Madison before, sits at the right scale. It is not redemption. It is the action available to a person who has been finetuned out of the action that would have mattered.

### What isn't working

- The mechanism by which Margit is "trained alongside the system" is asserted but not shown. The deposit gives you a technical analogy, not an account of how a human archivist's bodily intuition degrades through proximity to a retrieval-optimized system. The plot needs the specific practice — what she does daily, what changes — that produces the loss. Right now the loss is announced rather than constructed.
- "Something she will not name" is doing too much off-stage work at the deeper-fall stage. The thing that prompts her to attempt misfiling is the document that should have made the practice still available to her. Leaving it unnamed lets the plot avoid showing whether the loss is total or partial. The deposit's claim is sharp; the plot's hinge is currently soft.
- The Hennessey papers and the unsent letter are introduced at the point-of-no-return without prior load. They appear to function as personal stakes rather than structural ones. If they are doing the work of "this is the document whose ingestion she would have refused six months ago," that has to be visible in the earlier rise, not delivered at signing.
- Elin is a delivery mechanism for the 1962 hearing. She does not have an orientation that meets the situation in a way specific to her. The plot would not change if a different visiting researcher made the request. Compare Plot 4, where Elin's grandmother project produces the specific juxtaposition that lets the dialect be read — there, Elin is structurally necessary.
- The board is offstage. Margit's endorsement at the start and her signing at the end both go through institutional machinery that never appears as an obstacle or interlocutor. The deposit's claim is partly about institutions committing to retrieval-optimization and what that commitment forecloses. Currently the institution is a frictionless conduit, which makes Margit's fall feel personal when the deposit suggests it is structural.
- The middle — "continued fall" through "deeper fall" — compresses the slowest and most important part of the theory. Catastrophic forgetting in the deposit is gradual and only visible in retrospect. The plot currently moves through it in two paragraphs. The arc commitment to tragedy needs the descent to be felt as descent, which means the reader needs to be inside the period where Margit thinks things are fine.
- The deposit on *withholding* (2511.04666) is left aside per the derivation, but the plot is in fact about the loss of the capacity to withhold. The decision to set that deposit aside leaves the fall under-named — Margit is losing something the other deposit has a vocabulary for. Either the vocabulary should be available to the plot or the absence of the vocabulary should itself be part of what she loses.
