## Plot 1 — The Shadow Index (Rags to Riches)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Rags to Riches (rise) — fortune moves upward as Margit's secret misfiling practice, begun in private dread, accumulates into something she comes to recognize as a body of work; arrest falls at the moment the practice becomes irreversible because she has, without deciding to, transmitted it to a successor who has begun adding her own.
- **Papers drawn from:** Forgetting is Everywhere (arXiv:2511.04666); Catastrophic Forgetting in Mathematical Reasoning Finetuning (arXiv:2512.13706).
- **Picked up:** the third state — *withholding*, present-but-unretrievable; the shadow taxonomy as counter-archive; the propagation of the practice into a junior archivist's hands; the custodian's intuition as the system's externalized general capability.
- **Left aside:** the phase-transition / curriculum framing from 2503.21676; the science-fictional "AI dreams in her dialect" speculation; any explicit confrontation with administrators.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Margit is the custodian — twenty-two years at Special Collections, the woman who has lied to her father about her brother for thirty-three years and therefore knows in her body that withholding is not the same as losing. Marisol arrives as an external auditor for a Toronto-based foundation underwriting the AI preservation rollout in regional archives; her brief is fiduciary, but she is the kind of professional whose attention does not stay on its brief. Elin does not appear.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (low):** Margit has been instructed to prepare the Hennessey papers and the regional collection for ingestion. The vendor's promise — *the AI will preserve everything* — is repeated in three meetings. She begins, on the second Tuesday, to misfile. Small at first: a 1953 zoning variance into a parish bulletin folder. She tells herself it is fatigue.
- **First rise:** The misfilings find a logic her hands knew before she did. Records of her brother's generation — overdoses, evictions, juvenile holds — slide sideways into agricultural permits, weather logs, livestock census. She begins to feel, faintly, competent at something she cannot name.
- **Continued rise:** Marisol, doing a routine sample audit, notices an inconsistency. She does not report it. She comes back. She and Margit have a conversation about the affair Margit ended, which is not what either of them came to talk about. Marisol recognizes the practice without being told.
- **Key turn:** Marisol does not expose Margit. She files her audit clean. Then, in her own organization's records — a different archive, a different country — she begins to do the same thing with reproductive-health intake forms scheduled for AI ingestion.
- **Arrest:** Margit, weeks later, receives a letter from Marisol — actual paper — describing one specific misfiling Marisol has performed. Margit understands that the practice is no longer hers, that it has propagated, that she cannot stop it because it was never centralized in her. The shadow index has become a tradition. She files the letter in the wrong drawer on purpose. She is smiling when she does it. The story stops.

## Plot 2 — The Promise (Tragedy / Riches to Rags)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Tragedy (fall) — fortune moves steadily downward as Margit, having been the trusted institutional voice that endorsed the AI preservation contract, watches her competence be specialized out of her; arrest falls not at the bottom but at the point of no return — the moment she signs off on the final ingestion knowing what it will cost and signs anyway.
- **Papers drawn from:** Catastrophic Forgetting in Mathematical Reasoning Finetuning (arXiv:2512.13706).
- **Picked up:** *knowledge is not additive; learning something new destroys something old*; specialization-as-forgetting; the cost-of-competence; the custodian as externalized general capability that the institution cannot retain once it commits to retrieval-optimization.
- **Left aside:** the misfiling-as-resistance arc; the shadow-index propagation; phase-transitions and structured hallucinations; any successor figure.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Margit is the custodian who, *because* she is the most rigorously honest person in any room, was the one administrators trusted to evaluate the AI preservation vendor. Her endorsement carried the contract. Marisol does not appear. Elin appears late, briefly, as a visiting student researcher whose request — a specific document about a 1962 deportation hearing — surfaces what has been lost.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (high):** Margit's professional standing is at its peak. She is three years from retirement. She has just delivered the institutional recommendation: the AI ingestion is sound, the vendor is competent, the promise — preserve everything — is, with caveats she has documented, defensible. The board adopts her recommendation.
- **First fall:** Ingestion begins. She notices, in routine queries, that the system retrieves precisely. It also retrieves *only*. The diffuse civic intuition she had relied on — the felt sense that certain queries should return refusals, certain juxtapositions should not be performed — is absent from the system and, she realizes, increasingly absent from her own daily practice. She has stopped touching the paper.
- **Continued fall:** She tries to misfile, late, after reading something she will not name. Her hands have lost the knack. She has been trained, alongside the system, into retrieval-fluency. The general capacity has gone.
- **Deeper fall:** Elin, on a research visit, requests the 1962 hearing record. The AI surfaces it instantly and routes the query, per protocol, through the regional cross-reference. The descendants named in the record receive an automated notification. Margit understands that the old custodial discretion — *not this drawer, not this season* — was never a feature. It was her, and she has been finetuned out of herself.
- **Key turn / point of no return:** A second collection — the Hennessey papers, with the unsent letter she once wept over — comes up for ingestion. She is asked to sign off. She sees clearly what signing will cost. She signs. Not from cowardice. From the recognition that her refusal would now be incoherent: she is no longer the person whose refusal would mean anything.
- **Arrest:** She walks home. She lights a candle in the church on Williamson Street. She has not done this in Madison before. She does not pray. She sits.

## Plot 3 — Not This Drawer (Man in a Hole)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Man in a Hole (fall-rise) — fortune drops when Margit's misfiling is discovered by the wrong person, then rises into a different configuration than the one she fell from; the rise is contaminated by the fall because what she recovers is not her old position but a smaller, stranger one she could not have occupied without being caught. Arrest falls in the rise, on the specific exchange that makes the new configuration irreversible.
- **Papers drawn from:** How do language models learn facts? (arXiv:2503.21676); Forgetting is Everywhere (arXiv:2511.04666).
- **Picked up:** the curriculum / phase-transition frame — misfiling as rewriting the order of ingestion; *withholding* as a distinct state; the custodian acting on intuition without a theory.
- **Left aside:** catastrophic forgetting / specialization; the propagation-to-successor arc; the science-fictional dreaming machine.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Margit is the custodian, six months into the misfiling practice. Marisol appears as the foundation auditor. Elin does not appear. The friction is that Marisol's professional life is built on bodily autonomy and the language to claim it, while her own life is built on not having frank conversations. She recognizes Margit because she recognizes the structure.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (level):** Margit has a private practice. It is sustainable. She is not happy but she is in possession of herself.
- **The fall:** Marisol, on audit, identifies three misfilings in the sample. Her report is due Friday. She comes to Margit's office on Wednesday with the documents printed out. Margit, asked directly, does not lie. She has been the most rigorously honest person in any room she enters for thirty years. She tells Marisol exactly what she has done, including the part she has not told herself: that the brother whose name she has not said aloud in seven years overdosed in a motel and that the arrest report was the first document she misfiled, six months ago, on the day she realized the AI would surface it cleanly to anyone who searched the family name.
- **The bottom:** Marisol has the documentation. Margit has nowhere to put her hands.
- **The rise (contaminated):** Marisol does not file the report Friday. She files it the following Friday, with the misfilings unflagged, and asks Margit one question — not about the archive. About the brother. Margit answers. It is the first time in thirty-three years.
- **Key turn:** Marisol, on the plane home, drafts a letter to her own mother in Tagalog she half-remembers. She does not send it. She keeps it.
- **Arrest:** Margit calls her father on a Tuesday — not Sunday, not 4 p.m. — and tells him Peter died in 1991. He is silent for a long time. He says, *I know, Margit. I have known.* The configuration is new. It cannot return to what it was. The story stops there. The archive practice continues, unmentioned, in the background of a life that has just become smaller and more accurate.

## Plot 4 — Dialect (Icarus)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Icarus (rise-fall) — fortune rises as Margit's misfiling practice becomes, against her intent, legible to the system as a pattern it begins to learn from; fortune falls when the legibility is what destroys the practice. Arrest falls when the fall has become irreversible — when the misfilings have been "corrected" by a re-ingestion that cannot be undone.
- **Papers drawn from:** How do language models learn facts? (arXiv:2503.21676).
- **Picked up:** the science-fictional speculation — the AI begins to learn the misfilings as ground truth; structured hallucinations as the custodian's signature; *teaching the machine to dream in her dialect*; phase-transitions where knowledge is acquired or lost in non-smooth jumps.
- **Left aside:** the *withholding* register; specialization / catastrophic forgetting; the successor-archivist propagation.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Margit is the custodian. Elin appears as a seventeen-year-old visiting researcher — a Norwegian exchange student spending a semester at the university, working in the archive on a personal project about her grandmother who emigrated in the 1950s. She is the one who first notices the dialect. Marisol does not appear.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (low-mid):** The misfiling practice is two years old. Margit believes it is unreadable.
- **The rise:** The AI's query responses, over months, begin to show a pattern. When a user searches for one kind of document, adjacencies surface that should not be statistically likely. The misfilings have produced, in the system's learned representation, a structured neighborhood — exactly the *structured hallucination* the paper describes, but composed by Margit's hand. The system has not been corrupted. It has been *taught*. Internally, queries return richer, stranger, more accurate-feeling answers. Senior researchers begin to praise the new system. It is, they say, doing something the old archive never did.
- **The peak:** Elin, working on her grandmother's 1958 immigration trail, runs a search and gets back a result that includes a Hungarian-language poem fragment filed (by Margit, eight months ago) next to a customs manifest. The juxtaposition is, factually, what her grandmother would have wanted to be remembered next to. Elin notices. She does not understand. She asks Margit about it. Margit, for the first time, sees that the dialect can be read.
- **Key turn:** Elin, in good faith, mentions the strange and beautiful adjacency in a public-facing student blog post praising the archive's "curatorial intelligence." The vendor's engineers read it. They investigate. They identify the misfilings as data-quality contamination affecting model fidelity. A re-ingestion is scheduled. The misfilings will be canonically re-filed. The dialect will be flattened.
- **The fall:** Margit cannot stop the re-ingestion without revealing herself, which would also destroy the practice. She watches it happen.
- **Arrest:** The re-ingestion completes on a Thursday evening. The system's responses, queried late that night, return to clean retrieval. The dialect is gone. Margit sits at her desk. She does not cry. She thinks of Elin, who does not know what she did, and she does not blame her. The thing that was made could only have existed unread. Being read killed it. The story stops.

## Plot 5 — The Letter Ruth Wrote Back (Cinderella)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Cinderella (rise-fall-rise) — fortune rises as Margit finds an unexpected collaborator, falls when the collaboration costs more than she anticipated, and rises again into a configuration in which she is genuinely a different person than the one who began. Arrest falls in the second rise, in the moment her difference from her starting self becomes unmistakable.
- **Papers drawn from:** Forgetting is Everywhere (arXiv:2511.04666); Catastrophic Forgetting (arXiv:2512.13706).
- **Picked up:** *withholding* as a relation that withholds *from someone*; the custodian choosing on intuition without authorization; the cost-of-competence; the externalized general capability "walking around in a cardigan."
- **Left aside:** phase-transitions and structured hallucinations; the AI-as-dreamer speculation; the propagation-to-successor.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Margit is the custodian. Marisol is a professional acquaintance — they met at a digital-preservation conference in Toronto two years ago and have exchanged occasional emails. Marisol's organization is preparing to ingest its own intake records into a similar system; she contacts Margit unofficially for advice. Elin does not appear.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (low):** Margit, alone, doing the misfiling alone, drafting Ruth letters she does not finish. The practice is sustainable but lonely in the way of a draft from a window she has not located.
- **First rise:** Marisol's email. A consultation. Margit, cautiously, describes the misfiling practice — abstractly, theoretically, as a "thought experiment." Marisol, on the second call, says: *I think I am asking because I want to do this.* They begin a correspondence. Margit feels, for the first time in years, useful in a way that is not professional. She tells Marisol about Ruth. She does not tell Marisol about Peter.
- **The fall:** Marisol implements a version of the practice in her organization's intake records — files relating to clients who could be endangered if surfaced. A staff member notices and reports it as a data integrity violation. The board investigates. Marisol is asked to resign. She does. The funding for the regional programs she runs is paused pending review. Women in three countries lose access to programs because Marisol acted on Margit's transmitted intuition without the institutional cover Margit had.
- **The bottom:** Margit understands she gave Marisol a practice that worked for one custodian inside a stable institution and was lethal for the other. The cost-of-competence cuts both ways: Margit's specialization in a single archive made the practice safe for her; Marisol, not specialized in the same way, could not absorb the cost.
- **Second rise (contaminated):** Margit, for the first time in her life, acts outside the rigorously honest register. She writes a formal letter to Marisol's board taking authorial responsibility for the practice as a researched archival method she had recommended. She submits it. It is partly true. The board is unsatisfied but the letter changes the optics enough that Marisol's references are not poisoned.
- **Key turn:** Marisol calls her — on a Tuesday, for no reason. They talk for an hour about nothing. Margit, after the call, sits on the back step doing nothing.
- **Arrest:** Margit calls her father the following Sunday at 4 p.m. and tells him about Peter. She has become someone who can lie strategically to a board *and* tell the truth to her father. The two capacities, in her, were never compatible before. The story stops on the sentence she has not yet said but is about to.

## Plot 6 — The Hands That Knew (Oedipus)

### Derivation
- **Arc commitment:** Oedipus (fall-rise-fall) — fortune drops, partially recovers, then drops again at the moment of recognition. Arrest falls at recognition, not at consequence: the structural completion is the moment the protagonist sees what she has been doing.
- **Papers drawn from:** Catastrophic Forgetting (arXiv:2512.13706); How do language models learn facts? (arXiv:2503.21676).
- **Picked up:** *I do not know if she knows this; I do not think she has the words; her hands tell her not this drawer*; the custodian's intuition as pre-articulate; misfiling as rewriting curriculum order; the felt sense the system cannot hold.
- **Left aside:** the *withholding* terminology; the propagation-to-successor; the AI-dreams-in-her-dialect speculation.
- **How these people meet the situation:** Marisol is the custodian — not Margit. The plot transposes the seed. Marisol works at the Toronto nonprofit; the AI ingestion concerns the organization's case files from twelve years of programs across the Asia-Pacific region. She begins, quietly, to misfile. Elin and Margit do not appear; or Margit appears only as a name on a conference badge Marisol once collected.

### Plot
- **Starting condition (mid-high):** Marisol is competent, trusted, soon to have her funding renewed. The ingestion is announced.
- **The first fall:** She begins to misfile. She does not call it that. She "reorganizes" certain client files — survivors of trafficking, of domestic violence, of state action — into adjacent but non-canonical folders. She tells herself it is risk management. The practice grows.
- **The rise:** It works. The system ingests the canonical files cleanly. The reorganized files are present-but-unretrievable along ordinary trajectories. She sleeps better. She takes the diving certification course. She invites her mother to Toronto. There is a period — three months, maybe four — in which her professional and personal life align in a way she has not felt before. She thinks she has solved something.
- **The second fall — recognition:** A junior colleague, doing a routine cleanup, asks Marisol about a folder structure that does not match the schema. The question is innocent. Marisol, explaining, hears her own voice and recognizes the pattern: she is not protecting the clients. She is protecting herself from being *the one who decided*. The misfiling lets her act without authorizing the action, the way she ended the engagement to Andrew without authorizing the real reason, the way she has not had a frank conversation with her mother in nine years. She has built her professional ethics around bodily autonomy and the language to claim it, and she has been, in the archive, refusing to claim her own decision in language. The misfiling is the engagement-ending again. The misfiling is the unsent text to her father.
- **Arrest:** She sees it. That is the arrest. The story stops on the recognition, before the action it implies. The colleague is still standing in the doorway. The folder is still open on the screen. Marisol has not yet said anything. She is going to. The story does not show what she says. It stops at the seeing.
