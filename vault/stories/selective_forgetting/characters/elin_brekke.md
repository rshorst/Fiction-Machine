# Elin Brekke

## Face
A girl in her mid-to-late teens, riding a bicycle, photographed mid-motion but holding still in the gaze. Blonde hair loose under a white helmet, the strap clipped under the chin. White sweatshirt printed with "NYC 1998." Ripped light-wash jeans. One hand on the handlebar, the other holding a clear plastic water bottle. She is looking directly at the camera. The eyes are wide but not in surprise — they are wide in the way eyes are wide when someone is taking in a lot of information at once. The mouth is closed, neutral, but the lower lip is pressed slightly more than the upper. There is a small tension at the corners of the mouth. No smile is being offered. Her shoulders are square to the camera even though the bike is moving. She is not posing. She is not performing not-posing either. She is simply continuing to ride and continuing to look.

## History
Born 2007 in Bergen, Norway. Father is a marine biologist who works on salmon farms; mother is a high school chemistry teacher. One older brother, Jonas, twenty-two, doing a master's in Trondheim. Elin grew up in a wooden house painted dark red, ten minutes from the water, with a wood-burning stove that her mother kept lit from October to April.

She is seventeen now, in her second year of upper secondary, on the science track. She does cross-country skiing in winter and rides her bike everywhere in summer. She started reading her father's old marine biology textbooks when she was nine and never quite stopped.

Her best friend since age six is a girl named Saga, who came out as trans last year and is now Saga still, just more so. Elin has been Saga's most uncomplicated person — she did not make it a thing, and she did not make a point of not making it a thing either, which Saga noticed and was grateful for.

The sweatshirt is her brother's. She took it from his closet when he left for Trondheim. He has not asked for it back.

This summer, two months ago, she kissed a boy named Mikkel at a party and felt nothing — not bad, not good, just nothing — and then a week later she kissed a girl named Hanne at the same kind of party and felt something. She has not told anyone. She is still deciding whether what she felt was about Hanne specifically or about girls generally, and she suspects the difference matters and also that it doesn't.

Her grandmother on her father's side died last winter. They were close. Elin has her grandmother's small silver ring on a chain under her sweatshirt. She does not wear it on her finger because she is afraid of losing it.

## Desires
**Stated:** To study marine biology in Tromsø. To see the Lofoten Islands properly, not just from a car window. To get her driver's license in the spring.
**Unstated:** To find out whether the steadiness people see in her is who she actually is, or whether it is a thing she is performing so well she has fooled herself. To be in love with someone who looks at her the way her father looks at the sea — like the thing itself is enough.

## Contradiction
She is unusually undefended, looks people in the eye, does not perform for cameras, does not lie to her parents about where she is going — and she has been keeping a private notebook for two years that she has not shown anyone, in which she writes in English (not Norwegian) so that her mother would not be able to read it casually if she found it. The notebook is not full of secrets. It is full of ordinary things. The encryption is the point.

## What they don't know about themselves
That she chose marine biology because her father chose it, and that the love she has for the sea is real but is also a love she inherited rather than found. She thinks the path she is on is hers. It is partly hers and partly her father's, and she has never separated the two. She might never need to. But if her father's life turned out to have been a wrong turn, she would not, currently, know how to tell.

## Interview

1. **What is your name, and is it the name you would have chosen?**
   Elin Brekke. Yes. It's a short name. I like that it doesn't take long to say.

2. **Where do you live, and how long have you lived there?**
   Bergen. My whole life. Same house. Same room. The wallpaper is from before I was born.

3. **What did your parents do for a living?**
   My father studies salmon, mostly. He is on boats a lot. My mother teaches chemistry to teenagers who do not want to learn chemistry. She is patient about it. I would not be.

4. **What is the first lie you remember telling?**
   I told my brother I had seen a fox in the garden so he would come outside with me. There was no fox. He was angry. I was maybe five. I remember I was not sorry, only surprised that he was angry.

5. **What is something you are proud of that you have never told anyone?**
   I taught myself to fix a bike chain by watching videos in English with the sound off, because I wanted to see if I could understand without the words. I could. I was twelve. I have never told anyone because it would sound like I'm bragging.

6. **What is something you are ashamed of?**
   I was mean to a girl in middle school. Her name was Tuva. I didn't do anything big. I just didn't include her, on purpose, for a year. I think about it more than is reasonable.

7. **What do you do when you are alone?**
   I read. I write in a notebook. I ride. I lie on the floor of my room sometimes for a long time. My mother thinks this is concerning. It isn't. I am thinking.

8. **When did you last cry, and why?**
   When my grandmother died. In February. I haven't cried since. I don't think I'm avoiding it. I just haven't.

9. **What do you want that you would never ask for?**
   [She thinks for a while.] I would like to be told I am not a strange person. People say I am calm, or mature, or steady. I would like someone to say I am not strange. I know that is almost the same thing but it is not the same thing.

10. **What do you believe about God?**
    My father says the sea is older than any god people have invented and will be there after. I think that is probably right. I am not sure if it counts as a belief.

11. **Who in your life knows you best?**
    Saga. We have been friends since we were six. She knows when I'm lying even when I am lying to myself, which is annoying.

12. **What did you want to be when you were twelve?**
    A marine biologist. Same as now. I have not changed my mind. I do not know if that is a good sign or a worrying one.

13. **What is the worst thing you have ever done to someone you loved?**
    [silence]
    I told my brother that the reason our grandmother liked him better was that he was more like our father. It wasn't true. She didn't like him better. I said it because I was angry and I wanted him to feel the way I felt. He cried. He is older than me and he cried. I have not apologised. I should.

14. **What do you do when someone is angry with you?**
    I get very still. I do not argue back. I wait. I think this is sometimes worse for the other person than if I did argue.

15. **Are you lonely?**
    No. Sometimes I want to be more lonely than I am. I have a lot of people around me.

16. **What is your body doing right now?**
    My legs are tired in a good way. My helmet strap is too tight today; I will fix it when I stop. I am a little bit cold on the arms. The water in the bottle is warm now. I drank some anyway.

17. **What do you think happens when we die?**
    I think the part of us that is matter goes back to being matter. I do not have an opinion about the rest. My grandmother believed in heaven. I would like her to be right and I do not think she is.

18. **Have you ever been in love?**
    [The pause is long.] I do not know. I think possibly yes, recently. I am still finding out. Can I not answer this one fully? I would like to come back to it in a year.

19. **Is there something you are deciding right now?**
    Yes. I am not going to tell you what it is. But yes.

20. **Is there a question I should have asked you that I did not?**
    You should have asked me what I am like when no one is watching. I would have said: the same. That is the answer I would like to be true. I am not completely sure it is.
