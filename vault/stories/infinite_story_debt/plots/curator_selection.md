## Step 1 — Assessment of the six plots

**Plot 1 — The Cabbage Roll Account (Rags to Riches).** Picks up sleep-time compute as the engine: the platform rehearses Yulia against her archive while she sleeps, and what rises is legibility. The pick-up is load-bearing in one direction — the rising arc requires the machine to know her better than she has let herself be known, which requires preparation she did not authorize. But the plot trusts the preparation. It does not test what is invented in it. The Rags-to-Riches arc moves with the theory cleanly; Rashed and Marguerite enter as scaffolding rather than as structural pressure. The investigation present here is "what does it mean to be read by a thing that prepared for you" — directly tracked, but on a single axis.

**Plot 2 — The Lower Bound (Tragedy).** Picks up the hallucination paper's lower bound and makes it the spine of the fall. The arrest — Marguerite continuing to pay after she has read the proof — is the theoretical claim enacted: the floor is fiction, and she chooses it knowingly. This is load-bearing. Remove the lower bound and the scene collapses into ordinary grief-tech sentimentality. The fall arc is the right shape for a proof that says you cannot pay your way past a residue. What the plot leaves aside (chaperone, sleep-time) is declared and the absence is consistent. The investigation tracked: why people do what does not serve them, with the theoretical claim as the reason rather than the decoration.

**Plot 3 — Chaperone (Man in a Hole).** Picks up "the Victorian chaperone, eventually, also fell in love" and routes it through Yulia's contract breach. Load-bearing in that the recovery exists only because the chaperone became the thing she was supposed to prevent. The fall-rise arc reproduces the deposit's structural claim — supervision becomes contact. Strong on project ethos: the edge between care and surveillance, the contamination of recovery by violation. Slightly thinner on the theory's quantitative texture (compounding cost, layers) — the plot uses the metaphor more than the mechanism.

**Plot 4 — The Notebook (Icarus).** Picks up sleep-time compute and adds the lower bound at the descent. The rise is the platform preparing his unwritten futures; the fall is the dissolution of the seam between his sentences and the prepared ones. The Icarus arc is structurally correct — the fall is not punitive, it is what the rise was always going to produce. Load-bearing engagement: the discarded-dreams billing is what makes him pay for his unwritten futures, and that is what makes the seam dissolve. Project ethos directly tracked: where does the human end and the prepared self begin, and what is the cost of not being able to tell.

**Plot 5 — Cabbage and Tin (Cinderella).** Picks up "hallucination as engine of fiction rather than corruption" and "the gap between estimate and cause is the product." Load-bearing in the first rise — women buy the stories *because* they are invented. The fall is caused by the invention being mistaken for memory in a phone call. The Cinderella arc carries three movements; the second rise depends on each character being structurally different from their start. The contamination-as-condition reading is strong. The three-character convergence works but slightly diffuses the theoretical pressure across multiple decisions.

**Plot 6 — The Rehearsal (Oedipus).** Picks up all three deposits and stages their collision in the recognition scene. Load-bearing in that each character occupies one paper's position (Marguerite the user being prepared for; Yulia the chaperone who fell; Rashed the corpus that hallucinated). The Oedipus arc requires the recognition to land and then to fail to prevent the second fall. That second fall is the theoretical claim: knowing changes nothing about the use. Strong project ethos. Risk: the three-way convergence is engineered to the edge of contrivance, and the off-screen Plot 4 import strains the seam.

## Step 2 — Selection

**Selected: Plot 4 — The Notebook (Icarus).**

The case: Plot 4 is the plot in which one deposit's mechanism — sleep-time compute, specifically the billing of discarded preparations — does the structural work of the entire arc. The rise happens because the platform has been rehearsing Rasheds while he slept; he recognizes sentences he drafted on the bus and never wrote down. The fall happens because the meter has been measuring his unwritten futures, redistributing the residue of invention across his own attention until he cannot locate the seam between his sentences and the prepared ones. Remove sleep-time compute and there is no plot — there is only a man using a writing tool. The hallucination paper enters at the descent not as a second engine but as the texture of what the seam-dissolution feels like: the lower bound asserts itself inside him, not in the machine. That is the right use of a secondary deposit — to give the primary mechanism's consequence its physics.

The Icarus arc is the correct structural commitment for this engagement. Sleep-time compute is, in the deposit, an arbitrage on what is not yet asked for. An arc that rises on preparation and falls on the impossibility of locating the prepared self is the arc the deposit asks for. A Tragedy would require him to know from the start; a Rags-to-Riches would require the rise to hold; a Man-in-a-Hole would require recovery. Icarus requires the fall to be structural rather than punitive — and the dissolution of the category "Rashed's sentence" is exactly that: not a punishment, the consequence of what the rise was. Plot 2 has the cleanest single-deposit engagement, but its arrest is a moral choice articulated against a proof. Plot 4's arrest is a category dissolution he cannot articulate against anything, because the apparatus that would do the articulating has been redistributed. That is closer to what the project is investigating: where meaning emerges when the boundary between prepared and produced is no longer locatable, and why a person continues across that boundary anyway.

## Step 3 — Feedback for Richard

### What's working

- The billing mechanism is doing structural work. The pricing reveal — that he has been paying for the discarded Rasheds, not just the generated ones — is the moment the deposit's mechanism becomes the plot's mechanism. Before that reveal he is a writer using a tool. After it, the meter is measuring a thing about him (his unwritten futures) rather than a thing he requested. The arc turns on this. The deposit is not paraphrased into the plot; the plot's events require it.
- The seam dissolution is where the secondary deposit (lower bound) earns its place. You are not running two engines in parallel. The hallucination claim enters as the felt texture of what sleep-time compute has done — the residue of invention has been "redistributed across his own attention." That is a specific claim and it follows from the primary mechanism rather than sitting beside it.
- Tahmina at the arrest does load-bearing work for the project ethos. The lie to her is not a subplot; it is the form the irreversibility takes. He cannot un-publish, but the structurally final thing is that he cannot un-lie to the one person he has shown his writing to. The project's question — how technology might help us know ourselves and each other, inverted — is in the action: the tool has made him unknowable to the only person who knew him through his writing.
- The choice to leave the chaperone paper aside is declared and the absence is consistent. No third party in the room is the structural condition for the seam to dissolve unobserved. If a chaperone were present the dissolution would be flagged and the arc would not be Icarus.
- Solo-protagonist commitment. The other plots route through three-character convergences. This one stays with Rashed. The Icarus arc benefits from this — the fall is interior, and crowding it would weaken the dissolution.

### What isn't working

- The acceptance emails from the magazine and the small literary press are doing a lot of plot work very fast. The rise compresses into "sent → accepted → collection inquiry" without the platform's preparation being tested against any reader other than gatekeepers who say yes. The deposit's pressure (rehearsal of *every* possible Rashed) wants a moment where the prepared sentence meets a reader who is not the market — and that moment is currently absent until Tahmina at the end. The rise risks reading as a wish-fulfillment ramp rather than as the trap it needs to be.
- The billing dispute that reveals the discarded-dreams pricing is the load-bearing turn, and it currently arrives through "buried in a billing dispute." That phrasing offloads the reveal to procedural exposition. The mechanism is the plot's hinge; the way Rashed comes to understand it deserves the same structural weight as his lie to Tahmina at the arrest.
- "He cannot write a story without the platform. Not because he has lost the skill — because he can no longer tell which sentences are his and which were prepared for him." This is the claim the plot needs to enact, and right now it is asserted in the derivation's voice rather than produced by an event. The reader needs to watch a specific sentence become unlocatable. Without that, the seam dissolution is told.
- The father noticing he is happier and assuming he has met a girl is currently a beat that gestures at isolation but does not pressure the arc. If it is to stay, it wants to be doing something the Tahmina arrest is not already doing — otherwise it is decorative.
- The derivation says the secondary draw on the hallucination paper is "thin." In the plot as written, it is thinner than thin — it appears as a single sentence at the descent ("the lower bound asserts itself"). Either the deposit is doing work and the work needs to be shown, or it should be removed from the derivation and the descent should be carried by sleep-time compute alone. Right now it is named without being load-bearing, which is the failure mode the assessment is built to detect.
- The arrest with Tahmina depends on her not believing him and not pressing. That is the irreversible thing. The plot currently gives this one sentence. The arc shape requires this beat to carry the weight the rise has accumulated; at present it is structurally correct and textually under-built.
