## Face
Late 30s, possibly early 40s. The smile is wide and the teeth are very white, and the smile is doing more work than the rest of the face. The eyes are narrowed in a way that reads partly as smile-crinkle and partly as watching. There is a small asymmetry: the lower face is committed to the smile and the upper face is not quite committed in the same way. Skin weathering at the outer corners of the eyes and across the forehead. Hair swept up and back, styled with product. Yellow shirt. Leaning slightly forward toward the camera. The posture is deliberate. The gaze has a measuring quality — not cold, but not at rest.

## History
Born in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, 1985. Father a mid-level engineer at a state utility that became a private utility. Mother a pharmacist. He was the kind of child teachers liked — turned in homework, never the smartest in the room but always the most prepared. Came to Canada at nineteen on a student visa, did a business degree at Ryerson (now TMU), stayed. Worked in commercial real estate first, then pivoted into tech sales around 2014 when a friend told him the money was better. It was. He has been very good at it for ten years.

Married Alina, also Romanian-Canadian, in 2013. Two daughters, ages 8 and 5. They live in a townhouse in Leslieville they bought before the prices went stupid, which he mentions sometimes when other people complain about the market, and then regrets mentioning. He coaches his older daughter's soccer team on Saturdays. He is the assistant coach. The head coach is a man named Greg who Dan finds slightly ridiculous and wholly admires.

His mother died in 2019. He flew back for the funeral and stayed eleven days and did not cry until the plane home, when a flight attendant asked if he wanted ice with his water. His father remarried within a year, which Dan has not forgiven and pretends he has.

He is currently the second-highest performer on his sales team, which he is aware is the worst position to occupy.

## Desires
**Stated:** To make President's Club this year. To get the kids into the French immersion program. To start running again — he keeps saying this.
**Unstated:** To be told, by someone whose opinion he cannot dismiss, that he is not performing. That the warmth is real. He does not know which way he wants the answer to go.

## Contradiction
He is the warmest person in any room he enters and he keeps a private ledger of who has not texted him back. He genuinely likes people and he is also keeping score.

## What they don't know about themselves
That the watchfulness is not strategy. It started when he was eight and his father came home from a work trip in a mood that took three days to read. He thinks he watches because he is good at sales. He watches because he learned to.
