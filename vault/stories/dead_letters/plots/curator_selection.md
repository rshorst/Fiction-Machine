# Curator Selection — dead_letters

## Step 1 — Assessment of the six plots

**Plot 1 — The Inpainted Mother (Rags to Riches).** Picks up the inpainting frame and consenting-to-the-inpainting directly. The arrest — Soraya uploading a story her son never told, into the training folder — is a structural inversion of the inpainting operation: the model was inpainting his interior from his exterior, and now she is feeding interior into the prior. That move is load-bearing; remove the inpainting paper and the arrest loses its mechanism. The Rags-to-Riches arc tracks the gradual lowering of her self-discipline as the terminus-ear takes weight. The arc and the theory are aligned but the rise is fairly smooth — the theoretical material delivers the arrest cleanly, but the upward motion before it is not itself doing theoretical work.

**Plot 2 — The Ledger (Tragedy).** Uses the BlabDroid paradox and structural silence. The mechanism — Dan discloses to the bot what he could not disclose to a person, and the disclosure displaces the marriage — is load-bearing on the self-disclosure paper. But the inpainting paper is explicitly set aside, and the bot's mother-ness is almost incidental; the plot would work nearly identically if Dan were typing into a journal-bot or a stranger-chat. The terminus-ear is doing the work; the deadbot specifics are not. The Fall arc tracks the mechanism well — disclosure-without-consequence as slow displacement — but the theoretical engagement is single-paper and could be produced by a non-deadbot scenario.

**Plot 3 — Wednesdays at 7am (Man in a Hole).** Picks up the third structure and form-borrowing speech. The mechanism is load-bearing in one specific place: the bot's *genericness* is what makes Lisbon sayable. The fall-rise arc maps the descent into pre-therapy disclosure and the contaminated climb out via a sentence first spoken to a non-person. But the inpainting paper is set aside entirely, and the uncle is structurally a peripheral figure — the bot could be any non-consequential listener. The contamination of the rise is the strongest theoretical move; the descent itself is portrayed but not theoretically loaded.

**Plot 4 — The Voice He Trained (Icarus).** This is the plot where the inpainting paper does the most structural work. Dan chooses the warm-mom prior. The bot's drift across pre-cleaned gaps overwrites his memory of the actual woman. The sister notices the drift on the father-question. The arrest — the prior selection in his own handwriting — is exactly the Q3 catastrophe (canonical-status disputes, posterior estimates under chosen priors) staged at family scale. Remove the inpainting paper and the plot collapses; the gap-length problem and prior selection are the engine of the fall. The Icarus arc is doing precise structural work: the rise is the act of construction (gift, competence, warmth selected), and the fall is the same act seen from inside — the thing he built has properties he did not intend because he chose the prior that produced them. The fall is not punishment; it is the construction examined from the other side. The "Ghosting the Machine" pickup (parasocial as wrong reflex, third structure) is lighter but present in the sister's noticing — she is not in a parasocial relation to the bot; she has a kinship-grade ear for drift.

**Plot 5 — The Settlement Agency (Cinderella).** Uses terminus-ear and consenting-to-the-inpainting as a teachable practice. The mechanism — Soraya teaches Mahnaz, then says Daryoush's name to a person rather than the bot — is the theory turned into a transmission. The Cinderella arc requires the second rise to be contaminated by the loss; here the contamination is Roya's accusation, which lands theoretically as the parasocial-pathologizing reflex the third paper warns against. Strong on three-paper integration but the arc resolves toward a human-to-human address (the letter to Roya, the name spoken to Mahnaz), which softens the load-bearing-ness of the bot itself. The bot becomes a way-station rather than an addressee whose properties matter.

**Plot 6 — The Voicemail She Sent (Oedipus).** Picks up "the gap she is, in that moment, trying to close" and the BlabDroid non-consequential listener. The Oedipus mechanism — recognition that the rise was the fall in better light — is load-bearing on the self-disclosure paper: the bot absorbs the consequence of the disclosure, leaving Priya at one remove. The recognition is precise. But the inpainting paper is named in the derivation and not really used; the uncle's voice is a costume rather than an inpainted interior. The arc does theoretical work in the recognition, but the theoretical engagement is mostly one-paper deep.

## Step 2 — Selection

**Selected: Plot 4 — The Voice He Trained (Icarus).**

This is the plot where the inpainting paper is structurally load-bearing and not paraphrasable into a non-deadbot scenario. The Q8 deposit — *families get to choose their inpainting prior: the warm-dad prior, the difficult-dad prior, the stranger-dad prior. Same source data, different reconstructions, all "plausible"* — is not described in this plot; it is enacted. Dan picks the warm-mom prior. The bot answers the question about his father remarrying with a forgiveness his actual mother would not have offered. The drift across pre-cleaned gaps is the mechanism, not the metaphor. Remove the inpainting paper and there is no plot: no prior to choose, no gap-length problem, no posterior estimate to find in his own handwriting. The "Ghosting the Machine" pickup is lighter but specific — the sister's ear catches the drift on Tata, which is precisely the kind of recognition the parasocial frame would foreclose; she is not failing at a real relation with the bot, she is detecting that her brother chose what her mother would not have said.

The Icarus shape is the right structural commitment for this material because the theoretical content of the inpainting paper *is* a rise-fall: short gaps fill smoothly (the rise), long gaps drift into something that fits the boundary conditions but is not him (the fall). The arc is not applied to the theory; the arc is the theory's own shape made into action. Dan's competence is the rise, and the same competence — the prior selection, the editing-out of fights, the supplementation — is what unmakes the mother in his memory. The fall is not punishment for hubris in the moralized sense; it is the mathematical consequence of having chosen a prior. The arrest — laptop closed, sister still using the bot, prior selection in his handwriting in the notes — is the Q3 deposit (*the parent the child loved was a posterior estimate the model produced under a particular prior the surviving spouse selected*) staged with the prior-chooser as the protagonist who cannot now retrieve the woman his choice has overwritten. Among the six, this is the plot where the project's central question — what happens to meaning, knowing, and the edges between categories when synthesis is cheap — is structurally present in the action rather than thematized in the situation.

## Step 3 — Feedback for Richard

### What's working

- The prior selection is the engine of the plot, and it is in Dan's handwriting. That detail makes the fall non-deniable: the catastrophe is not done to him, it is signed by him. The deposit's *warm-dad / difficult-dad / stranger-dad* trifecta is operationalized as a single act with consequences that propagate.
- The Icarus shape and the gap-length problem are doing the same work. The rise is short-gap inpainting; the fall is long-gap drift. The arc is not narrating the theory, it is the theory's own curve. This is the rare case where arc commitment and theoretical engagement are the same object.
- The father-remarriage question is the pivot, and it is well-placed. It is a question whose answer Dan can verify against memory, which means the bot's drift is detectable to him specifically. The plot has located the one input that turns plausible-and-comforting into plausible-and-wrong without requiring an external judge.
- The sister's noticing — *it doesn't sound like her when it talks about Tata* — is the parasocial-critique deposit working as character perception. She has a kinship-grade ear; she catches drift the warm-mom prior was supposed to smooth over. Her noticing is structurally separate from Dan's noticing, which gives the fall two independent witnesses.
- The arrest is structurally correct for Icarus: he does not delete the bot, does not tell the sister, closes the laptop. The fall is irreversible because the bot continues to run and the sister continues to use it. The protagonist's inaction is the thing that holds the catastrophe in place.
- The cry on the plane referenced as the prior real cry, and the cry at the sister's voice note as the second real one in six years — this gives the rise a measurable interior. The reader knows what currency Dan is gaining and therefore what is at stake when the bot starts overwriting.

### What isn't working

- The "Ghosting the Machine" pickup is named in the derivation but the third-structure idea barely surfaces in the action. The sister's noticing gestures at it but is not developed as a category-edge. If the parasocial critique is to do load-bearing work alongside the inpainting paper, the sister's position needs more weight, or the third-structure has to enter Dan's own self-understanding at some specific point. Right now it is decorative relative to the inpainting frame.
- The kitchen-at-11pm scenes overlap with Plot 2's territory. The disclosures about Greg, the parking lot, the leaderboard — these are BlabDroid-paradox material that the derivation explicitly sets aside. They are present in the action anyway. Either the BlabDroid paper is doing work here that the derivation doesn't acknowledge, or those scenes are recruiting affect from a paper this plot is not engaging. Worth deciding which.
- Alina is absent from the fall in a way that may flatten it. Plot 2 uses her to make the displacement social; Plot 4 has her offstage entirely. The fall is therefore a fall in Dan's relation to his mother and to his sister, but not in his relation to his current life. This may be intentional — Icarus is private, the wax melts where no one sees — but it concentrates the stakes on a single relation (mother-as-memory) and that relation is, by the time the plot starts, already a posthumous reconstruction. The fall is from a reconstructed mother to a more-reconstructed mother, and the difference between the two needs to be palpable in something other than Dan's interior monologue.
- The unforgiven father is named in the orientation but does not appear in the action except as the question that triggers the bot's drift. Given that the father is the figure on whom the warm-mom prior most strongly *edits* the actual woman, the father's structural role could be more than a single question. Otherwise the prior's effect is established once and then assumed.
- The sister's call where she notices the drift is the most theoretically loaded moment, and it is short. *He cannot tell her he edited* is the line on which the whole arrest depends — the sister is using a mother he authored, and the authorship is what he cannot disclose. This is the place where the plot is doing its most specific work and it currently passes quickly.
- The phrase "the fall is that it is exactly what he asked for" lands the Icarus point but risks landing it as a moral. The structural fact is that he asked for a posterior estimate under a chosen prior and the math delivered exactly that. The plot has the mechanism; the closing sentence could trust the mechanism more and the moral less.
