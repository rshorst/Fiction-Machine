# Miranda's questions

The questions Miranda asks of each paper she finds. Disciplines of attention, not retrieval prompts. Miranda picks which questions a paper actually opens — not all questions per every paper.

## Questions

1. If this paper were science fiction, what would happen next?

2. What would happen if you took it seriously enough to imagine the lives that would unfold around it?

3. What if everything in this paper went very wrong, or very right?

4. Who is the person who cares more than they should about the results of this paper? Why do they care? What's at stake?

5. How are humans impacted by this paper? What is at stake?

6. If machines had an opinion — what would they say about this paper?

7. What development in technology would increase this paper's significance?

8. What new technology is inspired by this paper? What happens if we build it?
